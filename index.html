About me

Welcome to my homepage! I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at Purdue University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Paul Valiant and Prof. Steve Hanneke.

My research focuses on sequential decision making under uncertainty, especially multi-armed bandit and its applications to large language models and recommendation systems. In particular, I study how to make reliable decisions in unknown environments by balancing exploration and exploitation, with the goal of optimizing long-term outcomes rather than immediate rewards. This connects naturally to exploration strategies in modern recommendation systems and to how agents operate within LLM systems.

In summer 2026, I was a Ph.D. Software Engineering Intern at Google, working on YouTube Shorts recommendations.
